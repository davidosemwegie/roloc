# Ring Rush preview design

Bright and sculpted; genuine gameplay is the focal element. Paper #F0F6FC,
indigo #202344, blue #315DFF, orange #FF781F. Use the game's Rounded-Bold font.
Keep the game large, portrait, and uncropped. No simulated hands, fake UI,
leaderboard claims, or dramatic effects. The final caption sits in the existing
bottom margin, away from the playable board. Use an indigo caption on pale paper.

One uninterrupted capture with real in-game motion. No added camera motion.
The original recordings provide the audio identity. Frame timing is source time,
not the batch renderer's frame count.
